Where things stand

As of Oct 23. Eight sessions, 221 drives, two driver heads.

Best full session
Oct 21 · R9 after 9

226 carry / 246 total over 31 drives, 2,530 rpm, strike within 1 mm of center, face-to-path +0.5°. The first session where spin, strike and face all sat in the window at once.

Direction
Face, not path

Path is reliably in-to-out (+2 to +4°). The face is what moves: each degree of face-to-path is about 8 yards, and 68 of 81 big misses went right. The low takeaway and trail-shoulder dip both close the face to the path.

Strike
Centered vertically

Early sessions struck 12-15 mm low; Oct 13 after the tee change 6 mm low; Oct 21 centered. Horizontal spread with the shorter shaft on Oct 15 is the open question for the fitting.

Spin
2,300-2,800 window

Spin follows delivered loft (about 190 rpm per degree). The R9 sits in the window when struck center. The Qi4D short-shaft sessions delofted to 10-16° and dropped under 2,000 rpm, which cost carry.

Equipment
Regrip the R9 first

Standard-size grip, leave the length. At the GolfTec fitting: Qi4D Max vs standard at 12°, about 44.5", 60 g stiff mid-launch shaft (Ventus Blue 6-S class). Skip Ventus Black and the Cypher 50.

Session by session

Oct 21 · R931 drives after 9 sim holes · best 14-20Same R9, same shaft and grip. Centered strike, spin in the window, longest full session yet.

Spin and strike landed in the window

31 drives averaged 2,530 rpm, vertical strike within 1 mm of center, 1.43 smash, and the group centered on the target line (1 yard right on average). Carry averaged 226 and total 246. Shots 14-31 averaged 232 carry.

The face decides where it goes

Across 218 drives with club data, offline distance tracks face angle closely (correlation 0.81) and path barely at all (0.23). Path stays within about 1° from swing to swing; the face varies 2-3.5°. Each degree of face-to-path moves the finish about 8 yards.

Spin comes from delivered loft

Backspin follows spin loft (dynamic loft minus attack angle) at about 190 rpm per degree. Strike height matters less, about 36 rpm per mm. The R9 sessions delivered 18-20° of dynamic loft; the Qi4D short-shaft sessions 10-16°, which is where the low-spin drives came from.

Oct 21 · R9 warm-up24 drives, cold · best 10-13First swings of the day before 9 sim holes: low strikes, face open, spin high.

The cold warm-up looked like the old swing

The first 24 drives struck 12 mm low, spun 3,500 rpm and missed 22 yards right on average, with the face 1.5° open to the path. Only 21% would have stayed in a 30-yard fairway. That is the pattern to expect from a first tee shot without a routine.

Oct 15 pm · Qi4D32 drives after 18 sim holes · best 10-14Same move and shorter shaft as the morning. Spin collapsed: face delofted, strikes high.

Tightest dispersion of any session

32 drives averaged 19 yards from the line, centered 5 yards right, with half of them carrying draw spin. Face-to-path averaged 2.2° and the path was a gentle +1.8°. The move from the lesson held up without the coach watching.

Spin collapsed

Backspin averaged 1,970 rpm, down from 2,600-3,400 in every other session. Dynamic loft dropped to about 11° (from 17-18°) and strikes averaged 16 mm above center. Nine drives under 1,400 rpm carried only 169 yards despite 146 mph ball speed. Drives with 1,400-2,600 rpm carried 216 and ran out to 250.

Fatigue after 18 holes

Shots 1-13 averaged 222 carry and 250 total. Shots 20-32 averaged 179 carry. Shots 10-14 were the best five in a row: 229 carry, 257 total, 1.42 smash, face within 1.3° of the path. Shots 6 and 20 are sim club-data misreads and are left out of the club averages.

Oct 15 am · Qi4D17 drives · best 14-17Trail-shoulder dip to keep the hands low; shorter shaft; compact swing. Face started closing from shot 10.

The new move closed the face

With the trail-shoulder dip, face-to-path went from about +3° on shots 1-9 to about −1.5° on shots 10-17. That is the change the move was meant to make: the face stopped hanging open to the path.

Strike moved across the face

Six drives were struck 32-51 mm from center horizontally, all on the same side, and averaged 1.30 smash and 174 carry. The ten strikes within 25 mm averaged 1.43 smash and 207 carry. Shorter shaft plus a new move changed where the ball met the face.

Path is now reliably in-to-out

Every clean drive had a path between +1.4° and +6.3°, averaging about +3.7°. Attack angle averaged +6°, the highest of any session. Shot 6 is a sim misread (77 mph club speed, zero club data) and is left out of the club averages.

Oct 13 · R921 drives · best 14-2185-90% swing. Tee height settled after a 7-iron break; the second batch starts at shot 14.

A higher tee fixed the low strike

Shots 1-13 came before a 7 iron break, with tee height still changing. After the break he locked in a higher tee, and attack angle rose from about +3° to +5.5°. Strike moved from 15 mm low to 6 mm low, spin dropped from about 3,400 to 2,600 rpm, and carry jumped from 200 to 229 yards.

The push draw showed up

On shots 14-21 the path stayed around +3° with the face closed to it by about 1°. Six of the eight had draw spin, and they averaged 1 yard left of the target line.

85-90% effort, same speed

Club speed still averaged 100 mph, the same as full-effort sessions, and after the break it averaged 102. Shot 14, the first drive after 32 seven irons, went 238 carry and 254 total, struck 3 mm from center.

Oct 9 · R942 drives · best 33-40Deliberately dropped the trail shoulder to work on the push draw.

Shoulder drop moved the path way out

Dropping the trail shoulder sent the path well in-to-out: about +8° on shots 1-14. The face followed the path almost exactly, so the ball started right and never curved back. Those drives averaged 44 yards right. That's a push with no draw.

Dialed back, it was the longest stretch yet

Shots 33-40 had a path around +2° and the face slightly closed to it. They averaged 149 mph ball speed, 1.47 smash, 241 carry and 259 total, with an average miss of 16 yards. That matches the Qi4D's best stretch with the old driver.

Oct 6 · R934 drives after 9 sim holes · best 23-27Low, pendulum-style takeaway with deep hands to stay slotted.

Low takeaway fixed strike and face

Shots 23-27 came after adding a deliberate low takeaway. Vertical strike moved to about 1 mm from center and face-to-path tightened to about 1°. The session faded after that, likely from fatigue after 9 sim holes.

The baseline

The new head with the setup changes from the lesson produced a push draw on the best stretch, but the screen did not capture strike location, attack angle or dynamic loft, so this session anchors the ball-flight comparison only.

Flights are simulated from ball speed, launch, spin and spin axis using drag and spin-driven lift, then anchored to each shot's measured carry, apex and finish. Coefficients are standard approximations, not the simulator's own model. Average flights in Head-to-head mode are the point-by-point mean of the group's simulated flights. Dispersion ellipses are one standard deviation of the finishing positions, so about 40% of shots land inside them; dashed outlines are whole sessions, solid outlines are best stretches.
